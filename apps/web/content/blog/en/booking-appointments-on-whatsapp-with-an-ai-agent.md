Booking by chat seems simple: the customer asks for an appointment and gets a time. In practice, conversations get tangled when the agent does not know which service takes how long, who handles what or what to do when there is no slot.

## Define before connecting anything

- The services that can be booked and how long each takes
- Who provides them and in which hours
- How far ahead you can book, minimum and maximum
- What happens if the customer cancels or does not show up

## The minimal flow

1. The agent asks which service the appointment is for.
2. It offers two or three available slots, not the whole calendar.
3. It confirms name and contact phone.
4. It repeats the full appointment (service, day, time, place) and asks for confirmation.
5. It sends a reminder before the appointment with an option to reschedule.

## What the agent must not do

In health services, the agent books, shares hours and answers logistical questions. It does not diagnose, recommend treatments or promise results. If the customer describes a symptom or asks for a clinical opinion, the right move is to offer an appointment with the professional.

:::nota
If your business is health or aesthetics, also check your country's advertising and personal data rules before publishing the agent.
:::

## When there is no slot

It is the most neglected case. If there is no slot, the agent should offer the next available date or add the customer to a waiting list and notify when a space opens. Just saying "no slots" is losing the customer.

## Reminders that actually get read

A short reminder with the name, the day, the time and a quick-reply question ("See you Thursday at 4?") reduces no-shows without sounding automated. Remember that outside WhatsApp's 24-hour window you need an approved template.
