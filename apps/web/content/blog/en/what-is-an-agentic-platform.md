An agentic platform is a system where you create AI agents that serve your customers and, besides answering, carry out tasks: they qualify the contact, book an appointment or hand the conversation to a person. GenSmart is a platform of this kind, and you use it without writing code.

## How is it different from a chatbot?

A rules-based chatbot follows a path someone drew: if the customer picks an option, one fixed thing happens. If they write something outside the script, it gets lost. An agent reads what the person wrote in their own words and looks for the answer in the information you loaded.

## How is it different from an AI assistant?

A general assistant converses with whoever uses it and can talk about almost any topic. A business agent has a specific job: represent your company, speak only about what you taught it and leave the result in your team's tools. The difference is not how well it writes, but what it does after writing.

## What makes a platform agentic?

- <strong>Its own knowledge:</strong> it answers with your information, not guesses.
- <strong>Actions:</strong> it qualifies, books and records what happened in each conversation.
- <strong>Handoff rules:</strong> when it does not know something, it hands the conversation to a person.
- <strong>Several channels:</strong> the same agent serves on WhatsApp, Instagram and web.

## What is it good for in a small business?

It makes sure repeated questions get an answer at any hour and that your team receives conversations already organized, with the customer's details and an interest score. It does not replace commercial decisions: custom pricing, negotiation and closing remain a person's job.

## When do you not need an agentic platform?

If you receive very few messages a day and answer them yourself without effort, you do not need one yet. A simple options menu may also be enough when all your customers ask the same thing and nothing changes. The platform starts to pay off when messages arrive at any hour, across several channels, and each one carries different details that must be understood before answering.

## What should you be clear about before choosing one?

Ask three things. First, where the agent gets its answers and who can correct them. Second, what happens when it does not know something and how the conversation reaches your team. Third, which tools it connects to: your CRM, your calendar, your order system. If a platform cannot answer this clearly, you are not yet seeing how it would work with your business.
