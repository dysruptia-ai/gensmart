In a professional firm, the first meeting is wasted when the partner spends half of it finding out what the case is about. An AI agent handles the first message, explains your services with what you loaded, gathers the details your team defines and books with the right professional. It does not give legal, accounting or technical advice: that is done by the professional.

## What happens with inquiries that are not for you?

They arrive every day: someone asks about something your firm does not do or does not handle in their city. The agent explains which services you offer and for whom, with your own text. Whoever does not need what you do finds out before taking up a professional's meeting.

## What details does it gather before booking?

The ones you define by service. Someone inquiring about setting up a company can be asked about the number of partners; someone inquiring about another matter, about whatever your team needs to know before the first meeting. It all stays in the conversation, and the professional enters the meeting with that context.

## How does it book with the right professional?

- Identifies the service they are after from what the person says.
- Scores from 0 to 10 and places the contact in your funnel.
- Offers the free times from the professional's calendar, with the GenSmart calendar or Google Calendar.
- It can book with different partners, depending on the service.

## What does it not do?

It does not advise, quote fees or draft proposals or contracts. Fees are set by a person on your team, who receives the full conversation to do it. It does not receive documents on its own either: it works with what the person writes, and if you need documents from a system with an API, we look in the demo at how to connect it. It does not cancel or move meetings, and it does not write first. On WhatsApp it replies only within 24 hours of the last message.

## What changes for the professional?

They enter the first meeting with the conversation in view: which service the person is after, which details they already gave and what score they got. They do not repeat questions that were already asked and can use the meeting time for what justifies their hour: listening to the case, guiding and proposing. Partners stop serving first whoever advanced the least, because the score and the high interest notification order the queue. And since each inquiry is saved with its conversation in the GenSmart CRM, the team can pick up a case without depending on the memory of whoever handled it.

:::nota
If the agent receives a question like "what should I do in my case?", the right behavior is that it does not answer and books instead. Write that rule in your knowledge base and verify it in the tests.
:::
