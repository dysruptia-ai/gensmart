Someone going quiet almost never means they said no. It usually means they got distracted, had another urgency or were waiting for you to make the next step easy.

## Simple follow-up rules

- Pick up from the last thing the customer said, do not start from scratch.
- Each message adds something new: a detail, an easy question or an option.
- Ask something that can be answered in one word.
- Space the messages out. Two in a row on the same day feel like pressure.

## Where the limit is

After a couple of attempts with no reply, close with a kind message that leaves the door open and stop. Continuing to write is not only ineffective, it also hurts your brand. And on WhatsApp, outside the 24-hour window you can only write with approved templates.

:::nota
If the person asks you to stop writing, the agent respects it and records it. No exceptions.
:::
