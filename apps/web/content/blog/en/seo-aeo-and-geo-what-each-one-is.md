SEO aims to get your site well positioned in a search engine's results list. AEO aims to make your content the direct answer to a question. GEO aims to get generative AIs to cite you inside the answers they build. All three share a base: clear, useful and trustworthy content.

## What is SEO?

It is search engine optimization. It covers the technical side of the site (that it can be crawled, loads quickly and works on mobile), content that answers what people search for and trust signals, such as links from other sites. Its goal is to appear in the list and earn the click.

## What is AEO?

It is optimization for answer engines. It focuses on specific questions: you give the answer in the first sentences and structure it so the search engine can show it directly, without the person having to visit your page. It works very well with definitions, steps and frequently asked questions.

## What is GEO?

It is optimization for generative engines. An AI does not copy a page: it combines several sources and writes something new. GEO aims to make your business one of those sources, with consistent, verifiable and easy-to-extract information.

## How do they compare?

- <strong>SEO:</strong> where your page appears. Sought outcome: the click.
- <strong>AEO:</strong> whether your text is the answer displayed. Sought outcome: being the direct answer.
- <strong>GEO:</strong> whether the AI mentions you when answering. Sought outcome: being the cited source.
- <strong>What they share:</strong> clear content, consistent data and a site that can be read.

## Which one should you start with?

With basic SEO, because without a healthy site the other two do not work. Then write each page with a direct answer at the start, which serves AEO and GEO at once. They are not three separate projects, but one way of writing and maintaining your site.

## What work is shared across the three?

Almost all the work is reused. A clear page, with a direct answer at the start and question-style subheadings, helps SEO because it is easy to understand, AEO because the answer is ready and GEO because a fragment can be cited without losing meaning. What changes is the emphasis: for SEO the technical side weighs most, for AEO the structure and for GEO the consistency of your information everywhere.

:::nota
No one can guarantee you a Google position or an AI mention. Be wary of anyone who promises it and measure your progress with real queries.
:::

A good first step is to list the ten questions customers ask you most and check which ones have a clear answer on your site today. Those that do not are your first content list.
