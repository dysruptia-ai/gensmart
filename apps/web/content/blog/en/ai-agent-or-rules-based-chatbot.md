Both answer messages automatically, but they work differently. A rules-based chatbot follows a path someone drew: if the customer picks 1, this happens; if they pick 2, that happens. An AI agent reads what the person wrote in their own words and looks for the answer in what you taught it.

## When a rules-based chatbot is enough

- You have a few clear options that rarely change.
- Most of your customers ask the same question.
- You do not need to understand free messages, just route the person.

## When an AI agent makes sense

- Customers write in very different ways and with their own details.
- Your catalog, prices or policies change often.
- You want the agent to qualify, book or quote inside the conversation.

## The hidden cost of each

The rules chatbot is cheap to start and costly to maintain as the business grows: every new case is a new path. The agent requires writing what it knows well, but absorbs changes without redrawing the flow. In both cases, define when it hands off to a person.
