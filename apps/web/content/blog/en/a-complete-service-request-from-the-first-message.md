A technical service request can only be dispatched if it comes with three details: what the customer needs, at what address and by when. An AI agent gathers them inside the conversation, books the visit on your calendar and hands the complete request to your coordinator, who decides which crew goes out.

## What happens today with an incomplete request?

A message arrives that says "the AC broke" and nothing else. The coordinator, who should be assigning crews, ends up writing or calling to ask for the address, the type of equipment and the day. Meanwhile, the technicians are out in the field and nobody answers the new customers.

## How does the agent gather the request?

1. Greets the customer on the channel they wrote from and asks which service or fault they have.
2. Answers questions about services, coverage zones, hours and rates with what you loaded.
3. Asks for the address and the day and time slot they prefer.
4. Scores from 0 to 10 and offers the free times on your calendar.

## What does your coordinator receive?

The contact with their details, the score, the source channel, the booked visit with the address and the whole conversation. If the score is high, a notification arrives. With that, they dispatch without calling to ask the basics.

## Who quotes and who assigns the technician?

A person. The agent shares the rates and visit prices you loaded, but each job's quote is made by your team, and the technician assignment is decided by the coordinator. If you use service management software with an API, such as Synchroteam, we can look in the demo at how to connect it.

## And if it is an emergency?

The agent replies right away and collects the address and the problem. With a high score, your team gets a notification. Whether a technician goes out is always decided by a person. It does not announce that the technician is on the way either: it does not write first.

## What changes for the customer?

They get a reply right away, on the channel they already use, and do not have to wait for someone to come out of a visit to answer them. They are asked what is needed and nothing more, and the conversation ends with a visit proposed on your calendar instead of a "we will let you know". For someone with a stopped piece of equipment or a leak in progress, that difference weighs a lot. For your business, it means the request exists in full from the first moment, and the ones that arrive outside hours are waiting for the coordinator first thing in the morning, with everything noted.

:::nota
Define which zones you cover and which you do not, and load it as clear text. That keeps the agent from booking visits your coordinator would have to cancel later.
:::
