On Instagram people ask short things and expect a quick answer: price, size, availability, shipping. These questions repeat and almost always have a clear answer.

## What is worth automating

- Price, availability and sizes or variants.
- Shipping times and costs by city.
- Payment methods and sending the payment link.

## What to leave to a person

Order changes, complaints, large or custom sales and any conversation where the customer shows frustration. The agent flags it and leaves the summary so no one has to ask the same thing again.

## One conversation per customer

If the same customer writes on Instagram and later on WhatsApp, ideally the history is in one place. In GenSmart the six channels arrive in a single inbox and the same CRM, so whoever handles it sees everything before.
