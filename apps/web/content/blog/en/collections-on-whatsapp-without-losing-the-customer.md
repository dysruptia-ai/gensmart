Hardly anyone stops paying out of bad intent. They forget, do not have the link at hand or are waiting for a clarification. A message that starts from that idea gets more replies than one that sounds like an accusation.

## What each reminder should include

- The name, the item and the amount, with no detours.
- A single step to pay: the link or details, ready.
- A friendly tone that does not hide the matter.

## The rules you give the agent

1. It writes only within the hours you define.
2. It does not threaten or mention consequences you have not approved.
3. If the customer says they already paid or asks for an arrangement, it hands off to a person.
4. If they ask not to be contacted, it stops and records it.

:::nota
Check your country's collections rules before defining hours and contact frequency. This article is not legal advice.
:::
