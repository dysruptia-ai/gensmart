To take a new contact to an appointment in a single conversation, the agent does four things in order: it answers the question the person came with, asks the minimum needed to see if there is a fit, offers concrete time slots and confirms the appointment with clear details. The key is not making anyone wait between one step and the next.

## Why book within the same conversation?

Because interest is highest at the moment the person writes. If the appointment is left for later with a "we will contact you", the conversation has to be picked up again and some people no longer reply. Booking while interest is warm avoids that gap.

## What are the steps?

1. <strong>Answer first.</strong> If the person asks about price or location, answer before asking for anything. Nobody hands over details in exchange for silence.
2. <strong>Ask only what is needed.</strong> Only what changes the appointment: the reason, the service of interest, the city if you serve several locations.
3. <strong>Offer two or three time slots.</strong> An open question like "when can you?" creates back and forth. Concrete options can be answered with one word.
4. <strong>Confirm with everything in view.</strong> Day, time, place or link and what the person should bring or have ready.

## Which calendar does the agent use?

The GenSmart agent books on the GenSmart calendar or on Google Calendar, whichever you prefer. It sees the free slots and offers only those that exist. What it does not do is cancel or reschedule appointments: if the person needs a change, the conversation goes to someone on your team.

## What if the person does not want an appointment yet?

That is respected. The agent keeps answering questions, leaves the score and context in the record and the contact stays in the pipeline at the right stage. Pushing someone who is just comparing to book usually ends the conversation instead of moving it forward.

## What does a conversation that reaches the appointment look like?

It is short and orderly. The person asks about a service, the agent answers and asks a single qualifying question, the person replies, the agent offers two time slots, the person picks one and receives the confirmation. No forms, no switching channels. If at any point a doubt appears that the agent cannot resolve, the conversation goes to a person with all the context already written.
