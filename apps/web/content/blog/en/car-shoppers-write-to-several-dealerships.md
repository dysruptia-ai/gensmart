Whoever is thinking about a vehicle does not wait. They write to several dealerships the same day and talk first to the one that replies. An AI agent answers on the spot with your catalog, asks what the shopper is after, scores them and books the test drive, so your advisors serve first the person with the most interest.

## What does a person pricing a vehicle decide?

First whether the vehicle fits: type, use, budget and date. Then, who gave them a clear answer without making them wait. That is why the first replies weigh so much, and why an advisor who answers in order of arrival, not by interest, ends up calling last the person who was closest to buying.

## How does the agent handle someone who wants to buy?

- Answers questions about models, versions, prices, hours and locations with what you loaded for each brand.
- Asks what type of vehicle they are after, for what use, on what budget and by when.
- Gives them a score from 0 to 10 and places them in your funnel as a lead or an opportunity.
- Offers the free times on your calendar for the test drive.

## And the workshop?

If the inquiry is about service, the agent asks about the vehicle and the type of service and offers times for the workshop appointment. If your workshop system has an API, in the demo we look at how to check a vehicle's status and answer the customer. Without a connection, that question goes to your team.

## What about financing and the trade-in?

The agent shares the prices and conditions you loaded. The credit review, the negotiation and the close are handled by a person. With a used vehicle trade-in, it can ask for the details your team defines, such as make, model, year and mileage, and leave them with the advisor; the appraisal is done by a person.

## What does it not do?

It does not change or cancel appointments today: it passes the conversation to the team with the summary. It does not write first or send reminders. On WhatsApp it replies only within 24 hours of the customer's last message.

## How does the advisor's work end up?

More orderly. Instead of answering in order of arrival, they start with the high score contacts: those who have already said which vehicle they are after, on what budget and by when, and who have a test drive proposed. Each contact comes with the channel they arrived from and the full conversation, so there is no need to repeat questions. The advisor spends their time on what cannot be delegated: showing the vehicle, resolving deeper doubts, negotiating and accompanying the close. The rest has already been said and noted when they step in.

:::nota
If you handle several brands or locations, load each one separately. A shopper who asks about one version should not receive the price of another.
:::
