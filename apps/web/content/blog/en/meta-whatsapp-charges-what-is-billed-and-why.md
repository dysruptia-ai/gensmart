If your business uses WhatsApp through the API, Meta charges for the messages it sends. It does not charge per open conversation or per customer: it charges per delivered message, and the price depends on the message category and the recipient's country. The rules changed on October 1, 2026, which is why they are worth understanding before you plan costs.

## Why Meta charges

The free WhatsApp Business app is for businesses that answer from a phone. The API is the platform for companies that automate support or spread it across several people, and that one does carry a per-message rate set by Meta. When you use an artificial intelligence agent on WhatsApp, you are on the API.

The logic behind the categories is practical: replying to someone who wrote to you is not the same as writing to them first. The first is mostly free within an allowance. The second is charged from the first message.

## The four categories

Meta classifies every message a business sends and charges by category. These are the rules in force since October 1, 2026. The dollar value changes with the country of the customer who receives the message; to show the math we use Colombia's rates as an example:

- <strong>Service.</strong> The reply to someone who wrote to you, within 24 hours of their last message. Each number gets 1,000 a month free. From the 1,001st message of each month, Meta charges for each one: in Colombia, US$0.0008.
- <strong>Marketing.</strong> Promotions, news and offers. Always charged, from the first message: in Colombia, US$0.0125.
- <strong>Utility.</strong> Notices about something the customer already requested, such as an order status or an appointment confirmation. Charged: in Colombia, US$0.0008. Since October 1, 2026 it is also charged when sent within the 24 hours.
- <strong>Authentication.</strong> Verification codes. Charged: in Colombia, US$0.0008.

:::nota
The categories and the free allowance are the same for every country. What changes is the value per message: Meta sets it by the recipient's country, not your business's, and adjusts it over time. If your customers are in another country, look that country up in Meta's pricing table, which is the one that rules, and apply the same math.
:::

## When Meta does not charge

- <strong>The service allowance.</strong> Each WhatsApp number gets 1,000 free service messages a month. The allowance is per number, is not added up across numbers and what you do not use does not carry over to the next month.
- <strong>The window from a click-to-WhatsApp ad.</strong> If the customer writes to you from one of those ads, Meta opens a free window in which it charges no message category. According to the current documentation, it lasts up to 7 days.
- <strong>Messages you send from the WhatsApp Business app</strong> on your phone do not go through the API and are not charged.

## Let's do the math

Three businesses with round numbers and Colombia's rates, just to see the logic. Suppose about 5 service messages are sent per conversation. Your real figure depends on how your customers talk.

- <strong>A small store</strong> with 120 conversations a month sends about 600 messages. That is below 1,000, so Meta charges nothing.
- <strong>A mid-sized business</strong> with 400 conversations a month sends about 2,000. The first 1,000 are free and for the other 1,000 it pays Meta's rate. In Colombia that is US$0.80 a month (1,000 times US$0.0008).
- <strong>A business with two WhatsApp numbers</strong>, one with 900 messages a month and the other with 1,100. The allowance is per number: the first pays nothing and the second pays only for 100, which in Colombia is US$0.08.

Notice the difference between categories. In Colombia, 1,000 marketing messages cost US$12.50, while 1,000 service messages past the allowance cost US$0.80. What weighs most on the bill is not how much you talk, but what kind of messages you send.

## Who pays Meta and how

Meta charges the account that owns the number, with the payment method registered in its billing area. If you connect your own number to your own Meta account, the charge arrives there, directly.

:::nota
If the account has no payment method registered, Meta delivers the messages within the free allowance but not the ones after it. Replies would stop reaching the customer. Register the payment method from day one.
:::

## Meta and your GenSmart plan are two different charges

Think of a phone line and the person who answers. Who picks up is one thing and the network the call travels on is another.

- <strong>The GenSmart plan pays for whoever answers.</strong> That is the agent's work: understanding the question, finding the answer in the information you loaded, writing it, handing the conversation to a person when needed and leaving everything in the CRM and the calendar. The artificial intelligence models that do that work are inside the plan.
- <strong>Meta charges for the network.</strong> It is the cost of delivering each message over WhatsApp. GenSmart neither receives it nor sets it.

The GenSmart agent replies to whoever writes to it. It does not start conversations on its own or send promotions, so almost everything it sends over WhatsApp is a service message. That is why the Meta charge that matters day to day is the 1,000 message allowance per number. And if your agent works on Instagram, on your website or on any channel other than WhatsApp, this Meta charge does not exist.

## How to look after the allowance

1. <strong>Write the knowledge base well.</strong> An agent with complete information solves things in few messages, and every message you save counts.
2. <strong>Make use of click-to-WhatsApp ads.</strong> The conversations they open get a free window.
3. <strong>Check your usage once a month</strong> in your Meta account, especially before high volume seasons.
4. <strong>If you exceed the allowance every month, do the math</strong> with your country's rate and decide with numbers. Going past the allowance usually means there is volume.

## The questions that always come up

- <strong>Am I paying twice for the same thing?</strong> No. The plan pays for the agent's work. Meta charges for delivering the message over WhatsApp.
- <strong>If I change plans, does Meta's charge change?</strong> No. The 1,000 allowance depends on the WhatsApp number, not on the plan.
- <strong>Can the rules change?</strong> Yes. This is the version in force since October 1, 2026. Before, replies within 24 hours were free with no cap: today there is an allowance. Check Meta's documentation before planning costs.
- <strong>What about the other channels?</strong> Instagram, Messenger, Telegram, the website and email do not go through this Meta WhatsApp charge.
