To prepare for a high-volume date, update four things in your agent before it starts: the knowledge base with the season's offer, special hours, the conditions of each promotion and the handoff rules to your team. The agent answers with what you loaded, so if the information is old, so are the answers.

## Which dates belong on the calendar?

The ones that move your type of customer. In Colombia and much of Latin America, the most common are Mother's Day, Amor y Amistad, Black Friday, Christmas and the start of the year, with back to school and back to routine. Every business has its own peaks: check which dates in previous years filled your inbox and mark them ahead of time.

## What do you update in the knowledge base?

- Seasonal products or services, with availability and variants.
- Each promotion with its validity, conditions and exclusions, in plain words.
- Order deadlines to deliver on time and shipping times by city.
- Policies that change in season: exchanges, returns, warranties.

## What about hours and handoffs to people?

In season, people also write on holidays and at night. Define what special hours your team will have, what the agent leaves noted for the next business day and who receives the conversations when the score is high. If there is a day when no one can answer, say so clearly in the base so the agent tells customers when a person will be available.

## When do you do the preparation?

1. <strong>Well ahead:</strong> load the season's information and check that it does not contradict the usual.
2. <strong>A few days before:</strong> run test conversations with the most likely questions.
3. <strong>During the peak:</strong> review conversations each day and add to the base what the agent could not answer.
4. <strong>Afterward:</strong> remove expired promotions so they are not offered anymore.

## What new questions come up in season?

Questions appear that barely exist the rest of the year: whether it can be gift wrapped, up to what day an order is accepted to arrive on time, whether there is something for several people or whether the promotion stacks with another. If you do not anticipate them, the agent will pass many conversations to your team just when you have the fewest hands. Ask whoever handles service to note during the previous season what people asked and use it as a guide.

:::nota
The most common mistake is forgetting the last step. An expired promotion the agent keeps offering creates complaints. Note the removal date the same day you load it.
:::
