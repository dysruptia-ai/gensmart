An AI agent works in three steps that repeat with every message: it understands what the customer meant, consults the information you loaded and acts, whether by answering, qualifying, booking or handing the conversation to a person.

## How does it understand what the customer writes?

It reads the whole message, not isolated words. That is how it recognizes that "how much would Saturday cost me?" is a price question even without the word price, and that "I can no longer make it" is a change of plans. It also takes into account what was said earlier in the same conversation.

## Where does it get its answers?

From your knowledge base: the texts, frequently asked questions, price lists and policies you loaded. The agent searches there and writes the answer from that information. If what the customer asks is not there, it does not make it up: it says so and hands the conversation to a person.

## What actions can it carry out?

- <strong>Qualify:</strong> it gives the contact a score from 0 to 10 based on the conversation and places them in the CRM funnel.
- <strong>Alert:</strong> it notifies the team when the score is high.
- <strong>Book:</strong> it reserves an appointment in the GenSmart calendar or in Google Calendar.
- <strong>Hand off:</strong> it delivers the full conversation to a person when human judgment is needed.

## What happens when it does not know?

This is the most underestimated part. A good agent is defined as much by what it answers as by what it decides not to answer. The handoff rules, which you configure, mark when it stops and calls someone: a complaint, a custom quote, a question outside the knowledge base.

## Why does the way the information is written matter?

The agent is as clear as the knowledge base you give it. If your exchange policy is written in three versions that contradict each other, it will answer with that confusion. If each answer sits in a short paragraph, with the concrete detail first, the agent finds it and uses it well. Writing the base is the most important job in the whole process, and it is done by someone who knows your business, not by a technician.

## What does your team see after each conversation?

They see the full conversation, the details the customer gave, the score and the funnel stage where the contact ended up. They do not have to ask again what was already answered, and they can pick up the thread where the agent left it.

:::nota
To see how your agent thinks, test it as a customer would: write questions in your usual words, with typos and abbreviations, and see what answer comes out of your knowledge base.
:::
