Someone looking for a home rarely writes to just one agency. The one that replies first and clearly usually gets the viewing. An AI agent handles that first message with what you loaded for each property, qualifies the lead and offers times to visit with your advisor.

## First moment: "is it still available?"

It is the question in almost every message that arrives from a listing. The agent answers with that property's information: price, area, size, fees, conditions. If availability changes, you update what you loaded. If your system has an API, we can look in the demo at how to query it directly.

## Second moment: knowing who is writing

After answering, the agent asks what your advisor needs to know: when they need it, how many people would live there, budget and area. It does not fire everything at once; it mixes it into the conversation. With that it assigns a score from 0 to 10 and places the contact in your funnel as a lead or an opportunity.

## Third moment: the viewing

If interest is high, the agent offers free times from your advisor's calendar and books the viewing in the same conversation, with the GenSmart calendar or Google Calendar. It also does so at night and on weekends, which is when many people browse listings.

## What does the advisor receive?

- The contact with their details and the channel they came from.
- The score and a notification when it is high.
- The full conversation, so no questions are repeated on the call.

## What stays with a person?

Price, negotiation and closing. The agent does not negotiate or promise terms. It does not change or cancel viewings today either: if the lead wants to move the appointment, the agent hands the conversation to the advisor with the summary. And it does not write first: it replies when the lead writes, and on WhatsApp within 24 hours of their last message.

## When should the advisor take over the conversation?

When the lead already knows what they want and is ready to see the property, or when they ask something only the advisor can decide: a special condition, a negotiation, an exception. That is the point at which the full conversation goes to a person, with the score and the details already gathered. The advisor does not arrive at a cold call: they arrive to talk to someone who said what they need, by when and how many people would live there. That saves them the opening questions and leaves time for what is truly theirs, which is showing the property and accompanying the decision.
