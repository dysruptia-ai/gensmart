This glossary gathers fifteen terms that come up when discussing AI agents and business messaging, each with a short definition. It helps you follow a demo, compare platforms and explain to your team what is being set up.

## What does each term mean?

- <strong>AI agent:</strong> a program that converses with your customers, answers with your information and carries out tasks such as qualifying or booking.
- <strong>Agentic platform:</strong> a tool where you create and manage AI agents.
- <strong>Knowledge base:</strong> the texts, frequently asked questions and policies the agent consults to answer.
- <strong>Channel:</strong> the medium the customer writes through, such as WhatsApp, Instagram or your website.
- <strong>Multichannel:</strong> a single agent serving several channels at once.
- <strong>Unified inbox:</strong> a single place where conversations from all channels arrive.
- <strong>Handoff:</strong> the moment the agent passes the conversation to a person.
- <strong>Lead:</strong> a person who showed interest in what you sell.
- <strong>Qualification:</strong> the process of measuring how ready a lead is to buy.
- <strong>Lead score:</strong> a number, in GenSmart from 0 to 10, that sums up that level of interest.
- <strong>Funnel or pipeline:</strong> the stages a contact goes through, from first message to purchase.
- <strong>CRM:</strong> a system that stores each contact's record and their stage in the funnel.
- <strong>24-hour window:</strong> on WhatsApp, the period after the customer's last message during which you can reply freely.
- <strong>Template:</strong> a pre-approved WhatsApp message, required to write outside the 24-hour window.
- <strong>Embedded Signup:</strong> a Meta flow that lets you connect your WhatsApp Business number to the platform in a few steps and with no code.

## Which terms get confused most?

Qualification and score are used as synonyms, but they are not: qualifying is the process and the score is its result. Something similar happens with funnel and CRM. The funnel is the stages, the CRM is the tool where the contacts moving through them live.

## Why does the 24-hour window matter?

It is the WhatsApp rule that affects daily work the most. Each customer message opens a 24-hour period in which you can reply without restrictions. After that time, you can only write with an approved template. In GenSmart the agent replies within the window, so it pays to design the conversation to settle the important things while it is still open.

## What is the difference between a channel and a platform?

The channel is where your customer writes. The platform is where you manage the agent and the conversations. A single agent on one platform can serve several channels. If a vendor sells you a different agent for each channel, you are paying to separate what should go together.
