In business to business sales, the first message is usually short and vague: "need a quote for this". An AI agent handles it with your data sheets and catalog, asks for company, city, product and volume, scores the interest and books the demo, the visit or the call with an advisor. The quote, the negotiation and the close are handled by a person.

## Which technical questions can it answer?

The ones that have an answer in what you loaded: products, uses and features from your data sheets and catalog. A technical advisor no longer has to answer what is written on a sheet. If the question is not covered, the agent does not make it up and passes the conversation to a person on your team.

## What does your team need to know before quoting?

1. Which company is writing and in which city delivery would be.
2. Which product or concrete need they have.
3. What volume they handle.
4. Any other details you define: sector, timeline, contact.

The agent asks them inside the conversation and leaves them all together. Your team quotes knowing what each company is looking for instead of finding out on the call.

## How does it keep a long sale from going cold?

It scores each contact from 0 to 10 and places them in your funnel as a lead or an opportunity. Your team gets a notification when it is high and writes first to whoever is already ready. It does not follow up on its own: it does not write first, and on WhatsApp it replies only within 24 hours of the company's last message. That is why it is worth making the next step clear inside the conversation.

## What about the ERP and inventory?

It connects with practically anything that has an API. If your ERP or inventory system has one, we look in the demo at how to connect it. Without that connection, the agent answers with what is loaded and the rest goes to a person.

## And if there are several sales teams?

It can book with different advisors, and each one receives the conversation with full context. The demo or visit is booked on the GenSmart calendar or Google Calendar. It does not cancel or change appointments today: if the company wants to move the appointment, the conversation goes to the advisor.

## What changes for the sales team?

Each opportunity arrives with company, city, product and volume noted, and with a score that helps decide who to write to first. The technical advisor does not lose the morning answering what a data sheet says, and the salesperson does not quote blind. Long sales are still long, but they no longer go cold from lack of order: the full conversation stays in the CRM, and whoever picks up the contact weeks later knows what was discussed. The decisions that do belong to a person, such as price and terms, stay with a person.

:::nota
Load the technical sheets in separate pieces by product, with the use it is meant for and the one it is not. It is what most helps the agent not to mix up similar products.
:::
