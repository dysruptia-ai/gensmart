In finance and insurance, the agent does not advise or approve: it gathers and organizes. It explains your products and requirements with the texts you loaded, asks the details your team defines to quote or evaluate, scores the interest and books the call with an advisor. Advice, quotes, approval and contracting are handled by a person.

## What questions does your team repeat all day?

What a product covers, which requirements and documents are needed, how the process works. They are informational questions with a fixed answer. The agent answers them with your text, without giving anyone its own opinion about what suits them.

## What details does it ask for before passing on the inquiry?

The ones you define, depending on the product. For vehicle insurance it might be make, model and year; for a credit, whatever your team needs to start evaluating. It all stays in the conversation. When the advisor calls, they do not start by asking the basics.

## How is it decided who to call first?

- The agent assigns a score from 0 to 10 based on the person's interest.
- It places the contact in your funnel as a lead or an opportunity.
- Your team gets a notification when the score is high.
- Advisors call first the person who already said which product they want and when.

## What are its limits?

Clear ones. It does not give financial advice, quote, approve credit, issue policies or contract. It informs with what you loaded and leaves to a person anything that involves judgment about someone's situation. If asked something that is not loaded, it does not make up the answer and passes the conversation on. If your quoting system has an API, in the demo we check how to connect it, but the quote is delivered by a person.

## And the call?

The agent offers the free times on your calendar, with the GenSmart calendar or Google Calendar, and records the call. It does not change or cancel it today: if the person wants to move it, the conversation goes to your team with the summary. It does not write first or send reminders either.

## What does the person who inquires gain?

A clear and timely answer about what your institution offers and what is needed to start, without waiting for an advisor to have a gap. They also know from the beginning what comes next: a call with a person, who will explain their case. That clarity matters in a sector where people often arrive afraid of making a mistake. The agent does not pressure them or push them toward a product: it informs with your texts and books. And when the person asks something personal, such as which option suits them best, the honest answer is that the advisor answers that on the call.

:::nota
Write the product texts as information, not recommendation. "This policy covers X" informs; "this policy is right for you" is advice and belongs to your advisor. This article is not financial advice.
:::
