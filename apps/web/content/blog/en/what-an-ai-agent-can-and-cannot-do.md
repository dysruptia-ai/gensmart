Today an AI agent can answer with your information, qualify contacts, book appointments and hand the conversation to a person. It does not negotiate, close sales, collect payments or write to anyone first. Knowing those limits is what lets you configure it well and avoid promising your team something it will not deliver.

## What can it do?

- Answer questions with what you loaded into its knowledge base.
- Ask for the details your team needs and save them in the contact record.
- Qualify with a score from 0 to 10 and place the contact in the funnel.
- Book appointments in the GenSmart calendar or Google Calendar.
- Deliver the full conversation to a person when needed.

## What does it not do?

- <strong>It does not negotiate or close:</strong> discounts, terms and the final decision belong to a person.
- <strong>It does not collect payment:</strong> it can point to the payment methods you have published, but it does not handle the collection.
- <strong>It does not write first:</strong> it replies when the customer writes. It does not start conversations on its own or send reminders.
- <strong>It does not cancel or reschedule appointments:</strong> it books, but changes are handled by a person.
- <strong>It gives no medical, legal or financial advice:</strong> nor does it approve credit.

## What limit does it have on WhatsApp?

On WhatsApp the agent only replies within the 24-hour window counted from the customer's last message. If the window closed, it cannot write again on its own. It is a messaging platform rule, not an agent decision.

## How do you work well within those limits?

Design the work in two halves. The agent takes repeated questions, initial details and the first appointment. Your team takes negotiation, closing, collection and sensitive cases. When the agent hands over a conversation with the details already gathered, your team steps straight into what does require judgment.

## Why should the agent acknowledge its limits?

A customer forgives being told that a person will review their case. They do not forgive being promised something nobody will deliver. That is why an honest agent is worth more than one that wants to sound capable of everything. When it states clearly what comes next and who will handle it, the conversation moves forward instead of getting stuck, and your team receives the case with full context.

:::nota
Write a clear sentence in your knowledge base for each limit, for example what it says when someone asks for a discount. That way the customer gets an honest answer instead of silence.
:::
