If you use the WhatsApp Business API, sooner or later you run into this: a message that does not send because "the window has closed". It is not an error in your platform. It is a WhatsApp rule, and it is worth designing your agent around it.

## How the window works

Every time a customer writes to you, a 24-hour window opens. While it is open, you can reply with free-form messages: text, images, whatever the conversation needs. When the customer writes again, the window renews.

If 24 hours pass without the customer writing, the window closes. From that moment you can only start contact with a message template previously approved by Meta.

:::nota
Pricing conditions and template categories change over time. Always check Meta's current documentation before planning costs.
:::

## What changes for your agent

- <strong>Inside the window</strong> the agent converses freely and can close the sale, solve the issue or book.
- <strong>Outside the window</strong> it needs an approved template to reopen the conversation. The customer has to answer it for the window to open again.
- <strong>Templates</strong> do not allow free text: they carry variables (name, date, product) and go through a review.

## Three habits that prevent problems

1. <strong>Reply fast while the window is open.</strong> It is the part of the conversation you do not have to pay to reopen.
2. <strong>Prepare templates for the cases that repeat:</strong> appointment reminder, pending cart, quote follow-up. Get them approved early, not the day you need them.
3. <strong>End every conversation with a clear next step.</strong> If the customer knows what comes next, they are more likely to write back before the window closes.

## Write the template the way a person would

A cold template ("Dear customer, we remind you that...") gets fewer replies. One that sounds like someone on the team, short, with a question answered in one word, has a better chance of reopening the conversation. Avoid the notice tone and write the way you would write to a customer you know.

Example structure: a greeting with the name, one line about what was left pending and a closed question. Nothing else.
