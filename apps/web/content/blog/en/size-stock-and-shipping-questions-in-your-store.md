Whoever asks whether size M is left or how long shipping takes has almost always decided already. They are missing one answer. An AI agent with your catalog and your policies answers those questions on the spot, shares your checkout payment link and leaves to a person whatever needs judgment.

## Why do those three questions weigh so much?

Because they are logistics doubts, not interest doubts. The customer has seen the product and liked it. If nobody answers, they cool off while they open another tab with the store next door. And they write when they have time: at night, on a Sunday, on the way home. Your team is not always there.

## What does the agent answer with the catalog loaded?

- Products and prices exactly as you loaded them.
- Sizes, measurements or variants, if you included them in the product sheet.
- Your shipping, exchange and warranty policies.
- Payment methods you accept.

If the information is not there, the agent does not make it up: it hands the conversation to a person.

## And real time stock?

It depends on the connection. With the catalog you load it answers about products and prices. To know how many units are left, you connect your store through API: it connects with platforms such as Tiendanube, Shopify or WooCommerce, and with dropshipping platforms such as Dropi or Dropea. If your platform is not on that list, we look at it in the demo. Without a connection, the stock question goes to a person.

## How does the customer get to payment?

The agent shares your checkout payment link and the customer pays there, with your gateway. The store records the sale as usual. The agent does not take payment inside the conversation, and it does not write to someone who left a cart either: it replies when the customer writes, and on WhatsApp only within 24 hours of their last message.

## What stays in your team's hands?

Claims, returns and orders with problems. The agent hands over the full conversation with a summary, so whoever receives it does not have to ask again what happened.

## What changes for your support team?

With the repeated questions out of the way, your team focuses on what does need judgment: a claim, a return, an upset customer, an order that did not arrive. Everything also lands in a single inbox, no matter whether the customer wrote through WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, your website or email, and nobody has to check five apps to know what is pending. Each contact is saved in your GenSmart CRM with their conversation, so whoever picks up a case knows what was said before and the customer does not have to explain everything again.

:::nota
Before turning it on, rewrite your exchange policy in short sentences with no hidden exceptions. Whatever is ambiguous there is what will send the most conversations to your team.
:::
