To start with conversational AI in a small business you need three things: a channel where people already write to you, the answers to the questions that repeat most and a person on your team to receive the conversations the agent cannot resolve. With that, you can have a first agent running without programming.

## Where do you start?

With a single channel and a single goal. Pick the channel where most messages arrive today, usually WhatsApp, and a clear goal: answering frequent questions, qualifying leads or booking appointments. An agent with a defined job is easier to test and correct than one that tries to do everything.

## What should you prepare?

1. <strong>Your customers' real questions.</strong> Copy them from your conversations of recent weeks, exactly as they write them.
2. <strong>The correct answers.</strong> Published prices, hours, location, shipping, payment methods and policies, in writing.
3. <strong>The limits.</strong> What the agent must not answer and who it hands off to when it reaches that point.
4. <strong>Your calendar, if you will book.</strong> The available times and the calendar you use.
5. <strong>A responsible person.</strong> Someone who reviews the handed-off conversations every day.

## How do you test before going live?

Talk to the agent the way an impatient customer would: ask with spelling mistakes, change topics, ask for something that is not in the knowledge base. Note every answer you are not happy with and fix the knowledge base text. Repeat until the answers are the ones you would give.

## What comes after the first agent?

Review the real conversations of the first week. You will see which questions were missing from the base and which handoffs could have been avoided. Then add another channel or a second goal, such as scoring leads. Grow in that order, not the other way around.

## What mistakes are common when starting?

- Loading long, confusing documents instead of short, direct answers.
- Not defining who receives the agent's handoffs and leaving them unattended.
- Trying to cover every product and case from day one.
- Going live without having tested the agent with real messages.

Nearly all of them are avoided with a week of review. Look at the conversations, fix the base and adjust the rules. A small-business agent improves more through steady upkeep than through a perfect initial setup.

:::nota
Do not wait for a perfect knowledge base. Go live with the ten answers that repeat most and expand it with what shows up in real conversations.
:::
