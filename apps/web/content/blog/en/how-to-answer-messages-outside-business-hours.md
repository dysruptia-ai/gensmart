Many people write when they have time, not when your business is open. If the reply is an automatic message that only states your hours, the conversation ends there and the purchase goes to whoever answered first.

## What the agent can resolve on its own

- Questions that already have a fixed answer: published prices, hours, location, shipping, payment methods.
- Collecting the details your team will need: product, quantity, city, date.
- Booking an appointment or call if your calendar is connected.

## What it should leave for the morning

Anything that needs human judgment: a custom quote, an exception, a complaint. There the agent does not improvise. It tells the customer clearly when a person will contact them and leaves the case summarized in the inbox for whoever opens it first.

:::nota
Avoid promising an exact time you do not control. "A person from the team will write you tomorrow morning" is more honest than "in 5 minutes".
:::

## What a good night-time reply sounds like

It acknowledges the hour, solves what it can and sets the next step. No long apologies or answering-machine phrases. The customer does not need to be told it is outside business hours, they need to know what happens with their question.
