A team short on time cannot serve everyone at the same level. Qualifying orders the queue: who is ready to buy, who is comparing and who is just curious.

## The five questions

1. <strong>What do you need it for?</strong> It reveals the real use and avoids offering what does not fit.
2. <strong>By when?</strong> Urgency is the best signal of intent.
3. <strong>What quantity or size?</strong> It defines whether it is a small sale or a serious quote.
4. <strong>Do you have a budget in mind?</strong> Better to ask with tact than to find out at the end.
5. <strong>Who decides?</strong> In business sales, it avoids weeks of talking to someone who cannot approve.

## One at a time

The five are not fired off together. They are mixed into the conversation: answer something, ask one thing. If the customer gives two details in a single message, the agent does not ask for them again.

## What to do with the answers

What the customer answers is saved in their CRM record. With that you can assign a score and decide who goes to a salesperson right away and who stays in conversation with the agent.
