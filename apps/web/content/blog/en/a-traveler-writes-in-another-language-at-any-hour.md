Whoever plans a trip writes at any hour and in their own language. An AI agent replies in that language, with what you loaded about your tours, hotels and packages, asks for destination, dates and number of travelers, scores the interest and books a call with an advisor. The quote, the booking and the payment are handled by a person.

## Why do language and hour change the business?

Because the traveler compares agencies at their own pace and in their own time zone, not yours. If they write in English or Portuguese and the answer arrives the next day, or arrives in Spanish, they have already talked to another agency. Replying in their language and right away keeps them in the conversation while your team rests.

## What does it answer and in which languages?

It replies in the language the traveler writes in, whether Spanish, English, Portuguese or another. The information comes from what you loaded: destinations, what each package includes, schedules and conditions. If something is not loaded, the agent does not make it up and passes the conversation to a person.

## What does it ask before passing to the advisor?

1. Where they want to go.
2. On which dates.
3. How many people are traveling.
4. What budget they have in mind, if your team needs it.

With that, the advisor receives a request ready to quote, not a "hi, information" that forces a start from scratch.

## Who quotes, books and charges?

A person on your team. The agent does not quote, book or charge deposits, and it does not make changes either. If your booking system or payment platform has an API, in the demo we look at what can be connected, for example to check availability. Without a connection, those questions go to a person.

## How is it prioritized who to call?

With the score from 0 to 10 and the funnel placement. Your team gets a notification when it is high, and calls first whoever already said where they want to go, when and with how many. The call is booked with the GenSmart calendar or Google Calendar. The agent does not write first: it replies when the traveler writes, and on WhatsApp only within 24 hours of their last message.

## What changes for your agency?

Your team stops depending on who speaks which language and at what hour they log in. Inquiries arrive through WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, your website or email and land in the same inbox, with the full conversation and, if needed, in the traveler's original language. The advisor starts the day with requests ready to quote and a score that says which to prioritize. Whoever was just looking does not consume the time of someone who already has a date and travel companions. And whoever needs a person gets one: the call is booked on the calendar you connect.

:::nota
Write your packages in Spanish and check how it replies when asked in English. If an important condition is ambiguous in your original text, it will be ambiguous in any language.
:::
