A GenSmart agent can now connect to your Tiendanube store. Once connected, it answers with your real catalog: it searches products by name or category, shows the photo, explains the sizes or variants available, keeps track of the order inside the conversation and, at the end, hands over your store's payment link.

## What can the agent do with your store?

- <strong>Browse categories and search products.</strong> If the customer asks for «something for the rain» without naming a product, the agent looks at the categories and searches inside the right one.
- <strong>Show the product as an image.</strong> The photo arrives as an image in the conversation, not as a loose link.
- <strong>Explain the variants.</strong> Sizes, colors and other options come with their real name, such as «Size» or «Color».
- <strong>List without overwhelming.</strong> When there are several results, it offers them in short lines, with name and price, and asks which one to show as an image.
- <strong>Keep the order.</strong> After each product it asks whether the customer wants anything else before moving to checkout.

## How is payment handled?

At checkout, the agent asks for first name, last name and email, builds the order with what the customer chose and hands over your Tiendanube store's payment link. Payment happens at that link, with the right total and products. The agent does not charge on its own.

## How do I connect my store?

1. Create the agent from the Tiendanube store assistant template, which already includes the instructions and the details to ask the customer for.
2. In the agent's tools, choose the Tiendanube store one and press «Connect with my Tiendanube store».
3. Sign in to Tiendanube and authorize access. You return to your agent with the store connected.

If you switch stores or access stops working, the same place shows the connected store and a button to reconnect. This connection gets the agent consulting your catalog. Showing the conversation inside your store is a separate step.

## How do I test it before going live?

First in the preview, which does not use your plan's messages. These are the tests worth running:

1. Ask for a category without naming a product. The agent should explore categories and search inside it.
2. Ask for a product with a photo. It should arrive as an image.
3. Ask about sizes. It should list them with the option's real name.
4. Add two products in a row. It should ask «anything else?» after each one.
5. Close the order. It should ask for your details, build the order once and hand over the payment link.

Then repeat the same path on WhatsApp and open the payment link: it should show the right products and total.

:::nota
If you already have an agent created another way, you do not need to recreate it: connect the store and check that it asks for first name, last name and email, because the order needs all three.
:::
