For Google and AI to cite your content, start every page with the direct answer, use real questions as subheadings, back what you claim with original data that can be verified and keep your business information up to date. It is the same writing a reader in a hurry prefers.

## Why start with the answer?

Because a search engine or AI takes the fragment that answers the question, and almost always looks for it at the start. If your first paragraph is a general introduction, the answer gets buried. Write in two or three sentences what someone needs to know and then explain in detail for those who want to go deeper.

## How do you use questions as subheadings?

Write subheadings the way people ask: "How long does delivery take?", "What does the service include?". Below, answer in the first sentence and then expand. To know which questions to use, review the real messages you get from customers: your list is right there.

## What is original, verifiable data?

- Your process step by step, exactly as you do it.
- Real coverage areas, hours, terms and policies.
- The criteria you use to decide, for example when you recommend one option over another.
- Information only you can give and that anyone can check with you.

What does not work is inventing figures or filler phrases. A claim you cannot support erodes trust, both with people and with the systems that decide whom to cite.

## Why does the business profile matter?

Because it is one of the sources consulted to confirm that you exist and what you do. If your profile says one schedule and your site another, or carries an old address, you create doubt. Check name, address, phone, hours and services on your profile, your site and your social accounts, and make sure they match. If a digital marketing agency manages it for you, ask them to review it regularly.

## How do you know if it works?

Ask the questions your customers would ask, in a search engine and in an AI, and see what shows up. Note whether you are mentioned, what is said about you and whether the information is correct. Repeat every so often and compare. Do not expect results overnight: clear content accumulates, and each well-written page adds to the previous ones.

:::nota
Pick your most important service page today and rewrite only its first paragraph as a direct answer of two or three sentences. It is the small change with the most effect.
:::

When you finish that page, apply the same method to the next one on your list. With a few well-made pages, your site already answers better than a long, vague one.
